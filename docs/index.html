Leaders

Each leader is a new, separately selectable leader that stands beside the stock ones. Four are built for expansion (Mali, Ethiopia, Cree, Russia) and four for Wonders, Preserves and Appeal (China, Brazil, America, France).

  • As designed matches the design brief
  • Adjusted the game engine forced a change, explained underneath
  • Added not in the brief, needed for BBG
M

Golden Expansion

Mali · expansion
  • As designed

    Settlers cost 10% less Gold to purchase.

  • Adjusted

    Domestic Trade Routes sent from a city with a Suguba provide +2 Production.

    The brief gave the Production to the destination city. Civ VI pays domestic route yields to the city that sends the route, and has no modifier that pays the receiving city, so the sending Suguba city gets it.

  • Adjusted

    +1 Trade Route capacity for each continent on which you own at least three cities that you founded.

    Capped at +4, where the brief had no cap. Counted by script at the start of your turn and whenever a city is founded or captured. Captured cities do not count.

  • Added

    Sugubas gain +2 Gold for each adjacent Holy Site.

    This is Mansa Musa's Suguba bonus in BBG. BBG gives each stock Mali leader one and switches it off for the other, so a new Mali leader would have received both. This leader keeps the Holy Site one and opts out of Sundiata Keita's Theater Square one.

Unchanged: Songs of the Jeli, the Suguba and the Mandekalu Cavalry, exactly as BBG 7.5.1 defines them.
C

Imperial Gardens

China · Wonders and Appeal
  • Adjusted

    Every tile of a city that holds at least one Wonder has +1 Appeal.

    The brief gave +1 Appeal only to the tiles next to each Wonder. Civ VI has no modifier that changes Appeal on chosen tiles, only on a whole city. This version covers more tiles but does not stack when two Wonders share a neighbour.

  • As designed

    In a city with a Grove: +5% Production toward Wonders for each Breathtaking tile next to the Wonder being built, up to +20%.

    Counted by script at the start of your turn, so a Wonder placed this turn starts earning the bonus next turn.

  • Added

    Great Wall segments next to another segment provide +1 Faith.

    BBG gives every Chinese leader their own yield here, and a new leader would have had none. The others: Qin (Mandate of Heaven) Faith and Gold, Qin (Unifier) Production, Wu Zetian Science, Yongle Food, Kublai Khan Culture. Faith was chosen to feed Preserves and Groves.

Unchanged: Dynastic Cycle (Eurekas and Inspirations as BBG sets them), the Great Wall and the Crouching Tiger.Not included: Qin's extra Builder charge and Builder-rushed Wonders. Those belong to his leader ability.
E

Highland Empire

Ethiopia · expansion · needs the Ethiopia Pack
  • As designed

    Cities you found on Hills gain +1 Production.

  • As designed

    Those cities also gain +10% Production toward Settlers.

  • As designed

    Those cities gain +25% Production toward districts until their first specialty district is complete.

    While the city has no specialty district the bonus also applies to non-specialty districts such as an Aqueduct.

Unchanged: Aksumite Legacy, the Rock-Hewn Church and the Oromo Cavalry.Not included: Menelik's Council of Ministers (Science and Culture from Faith in Hill cities).
B

Living Paradise

Brazil · Wonders and Appeal
  • As designed

    Unimproved Rainforest next to a Preserve provides +1 Culture once Medieval Faires is known.

  • Adjusted

    Cities with at least three Rainforest tiles gain +10% Production toward Wonders.

    The brief counted only unimproved Rainforest. The game's own city test counts every Rainforest tile the city owns, improved or not.

  • Adjusted

    Once Medieval Faires is known, each Rainforest in a city that holds at least one Wonder gives a further +1 Appeal to the tiles around it.

    The brief raised the Appeal of Rainforest tiles next to each finished Wonder. With no per-tile Appeal modifier in the engine, this raises what Rainforest radiates instead, on top of Brazil's existing +1, across any city that has a Wonder. It waits for Medieval Faires because, from the first Wonder, it made early Grove tiles Breathtaking and Culture rose too fast.

Unchanged: Amazon (including BBG's +1 Production on unimproved Rainforest after Construction), the Street Carnival and the Minas Geraes.Not included: Pedro's Magnanimous (Great Person point refunds).
Cr

Borderless Empire

Cree · expansion · needs Rise and Fall
  • As designed

    Settlers have +1 sight.

  • As designed

    The first Trade Route sent from each city you found claims up to two unowned tiles near that city.

    Done by script at the start of your turn, so the tiles arrive the turn after the Trader sets out. It takes the nearest unowned tiles within three of the city centre, land before water. Once per city; captured cities do not count.

  • As designed

    International Trade Routes sent from a city with at least one Camp or Pasture provide +2 Food and +1 Production.

    Whether a city has a Camp or Pasture is checked by script at the start of your turn.

Unchanged: Nihithaw (Traders still claim the tiles they walk through), the Mekewap and the Okihtcitaw.Not included: Poundmaker's Favorable Terms (shared visibility with allies, and Food and Gold for Camps and Pastures in the destination city).
R

Endless Continent

Russia · expansion
  • As designed

    Cities with a Lavra gain +20% Production toward Settlers.

  • As designed

    Cities you found on Tundra or Tundra Hills receive a free Builder.

    This includes your capital if it starts on Tundra.

  • As designed

    Tundra Woods provide +1 Food.

Unchanged: Mother Russia (extra territory on founding, and the Tundra yields as BBG sets them), the Lavra and the Cossack.Not included: Peter's Grand Embassy (Science and Culture from Trade Routes to more advanced civilizations).
A

Scenic Genius

America · Wonders and Appeal
  • As designed

    +30% Production toward Preserves.

  • As designed

    +10% Production toward a Wonder that is next to a Breathtaking tile.

    Counted by script at the start of your turn, so a Wonder placed this turn starts earning the bonus next turn.

  • As designed

    Unimproved Breathtaking tiles next to a Wonder provide +1 Culture.

  • As designed

    Every tile of a city that holds a National Park has +1 Appeal.

Unchanged: Founding Fathers, the Film Studio and the P-51 Mustang.Not included: Teddy Roosevelt's own abilities in either persona (the Bull Moose yields on Breathtaking tiles, the Roosevelt Corollary and the Rough Rider).
F

Palace Designer

France · Wonders and Appeal
  • Adjusted

    Every tile of a city in which a Château is next to a Wonder or a Preserve has +1 Appeal.

    The brief gave +1 more Appeal to the tiles next to each Château. A Château's Appeal is one number shared by every French leader, and Civ VI has no modifier that changes Appeal on chosen tiles, only on a whole city. So the bonus covers the whole city, and to keep that from being too cheap it only switches on once a Château stands beside a finished Wonder or a Preserve. Checked by script at the start of your turn; it does not stack.

  • As designed

    Châteaux next to a Wonder or a Preserve provide +1 Production.

    +1 in total, not +1 for each Wonder or Preserve beside it.

  • As designed

    +10% Production toward a Wonder that is next to a Château.

    Counted by script at the start of your turn, so a Wonder placed this turn starts earning the bonus next turn.

Unchanged: Grand Tour, the Château and the Garde Impériale.Not included: Catherine de Medici's abilities in either persona (Flying Squadron, Magnificences).

City-states

Six new city-states, one of each type, that join the pool the game draws from and appear in the city-state picker. Each gives its type's usual envoy bonuses at 1, 3 and 6 envoys, plus the Suzerain bonus below. Gathering Storm ruleset only. With BBG they also count toward its per-city-state bonuses (Pericles, the Huszar, the Varangian longship).

S

Syracuse

Scientific · Applied Engineering
  • Adjusted

    Campuses receive +1 Science for each adjacent Aqueduct or Dam.

    The brief asked for true district adjacency. Civ VI's district-adjacency modifier cannot name the neighbouring district (it counts any district), so a script counts the neighbours and the yield is added to the district itself. It does not appear on the adjacency line of the tooltip, and whether adjacency-doubling policy cards multiply it has not been tested in game. Counted at the start of your turn and when a district is finished. Each Aqueduct and Dam counts separately; a Bath counts as an Aqueduct.

  • As designed

    Workshops provide +1 Science in cities with a Campus.

    Once per Workshop, wherever the two districts stand.

U

Ubud

Cultural · Art in Nature
  • Adjusted

    Theater Squares receive +1 Culture for each adjacent unimproved Woods or Rainforest, to a maximum of +2.

    Added to the district rather than declared as adjacency, for the same reason as Syracuse: the engine's feature-adjacency modifier can neither skip improved tiles nor stop at two. The tiles need not belong to the same city, or to anyone.

  • As designed

    After Flight, Theater Squares next to at least two such tiles provide +2 Tourism.

    A flat +2 per Theater Square, not per tile.

S

Sheffield

Industrial · Masters of Steel
  • As designed

    Mines and Quarries next to one of your Industrial Zones receive +1 Production.

    +1 in total, however many Zones the tile touches. The Zone must be yours and finished; a Hansa or Oppidum counts. Flagged by script at the start of your turn, so a Mine built this turn earns it next turn.

  • As designed

    Each Industrial Zone with a Workshop and at least three adjacent Mines or Quarries provides +1 Great Engineer point per turn.

    No empire-wide cap. The Mines and Quarries must be yours and unpillaged but may belong to another of your cities; the Zone must be finished and unpillaged.

R

Ravenna

Militaristic · Imperial Engineering
  • Adjusted

    +20% Production toward Harbor and Encampment districts.

    The brief gave Military Engineers +1 build charge. Neither of us builds them, so the bonus now speeds up the two districts the second half asks for. Unique Harbors and Encampments (Royal Navy Dockyard, Cothon, Ikanda, Thành) get it too.

  • Adjusted

    Cities with both a Harbor and an Encampment gain +2 Production while constructing a building of either district.

    Civ VI only has percentage bonuses toward buildings, so this is +2 city Production that a script switches on while a Harbor or Encampment building is what the city is making. The script looks at the start of your turn: if you change production mid-turn, the bonus follows one turn later.

L

Lhasa

Religious · Mountain Monasteries
  • As designed

    Holy Sites adjacent to at least two Mountains provide +2 Culture.

    A flat district yield; more Mountains add nothing. The Mountains can be anyone's or no one's.

  • As designed

    Shrines in those cities provide +1 Production.

B

Bruges

Trade · Mercantile Renaissance
  • Adjusted

    Commercial Hubs receive +1 Gold for each adjacent Theater Square, and Theater Squares receive +1 Culture for each adjacent Commercial Hub.

    Added to the district rather than declared as adjacency, for the same reason as Syracuse.

  • As designed

    Cities with both districts gain +1 Culture for each Trade Route they send, to a maximum of +2 per city.

    Domestic and international routes both count, the districts need not touch, and each city is capped on its own. Counted at the start of your turn.

Pantheons

Six new pantheons, offered alongside the stock and BBG ones; nothing is replaced. Any civilization can take them, the bonus belongs to whoever holds the pantheon (it does not depend on what an individual city follows) and it carries on after a religion is founded. None has an empire-wide cap. With the Spare Beliefs mod both players can take the same one. Gathering Storm ruleset only. Every effect is an ordinary modifier; a small script supplies two facts the modifiers cannot work out (whose Preserve a tile touches, and whether a city's Wonder is finished).

H

Hearths of the First Settlers

Pantheon · expansion
  • As designed

    Cities you founded receive +1 Food and +1 Production while they have 1 to 3 Population.

    A city yield, not a City Centre tile yield. Includes your Capital and cities founded before you took the pantheon. Captured cities never qualify. Switches off at 4 Population and back on if the city shrinks below 4.

S

Sacred Companions

Pantheon · luxuries
  • As designed

    Camps over Peacocks, Pallas's Cats, Pygmy Rabbits, Tibetan Foxes and Capybaras provide +1 Production and +1 Faith.

    Added to the tile. The five resources are an explicit list checked against the resource pack: every one is improved by a Camp. Topaz (Mine) and Discus Fish (Fishing Boats) are not on it. If a listed resource is ever removed, this pantheon is not offered at all rather than offered and doing nothing. Stacks with Goddess of the Hunt.

G

Guilds of the New Realm

Pantheon · infrastructure
  • As designed

    Builders trained or purchased in a city with a Market receive +1 build charge.

    Built the same way as Liang's Guildmaster promotion: given once when the Builder is made, kept if the Market is later pillaged, never added to Builders that already exist, and adds to Liang, Serfdom, Public Works and the Pyramids. A free Builder that appears in such a city may also get it; that is accepted.

W

Sacred Wilderness

Pantheon · Appeal
  • As designed

    Once you have Medieval Faires, unimproved Breathtaking tiles next to a Preserve provide +1 Production.

    A tile yield, so a tile gets it once however many Preserves touch it, and it follows Appeal, improvements and districts as they change. Tiles holding a district, Wonder or City Centre never qualify. The Preserve must be yours, finished and unpillaged; the other player's Preserve on your border does not count. The engine's own adjacency test cannot tell whose Preserve it is, so a script marks the tiles: that one fact is refreshed at the start of your turn, when a district is finished, when something is pillaged and when a city changes hands. The tile and the Preserve need not be in the same city.

D

Divine Inspiration

Pantheon · Wonders and culture
  • As designed

    Cities with an Art Museum and at least one World Wonder generate +1 Great Artist point per turn.

    Once per city however many Wonders it has; a captured Wonder counts; the museum can be empty; an Archaeological Museum does not count. Shares its name with the stock Follower belief that gives Faith from Wonders. That belief is untouched, the two are separate, and you can hold both. A Wonder still under construction does not count: a script marks a city once its Wonder is finished.

P

Pillars of Creation

Pantheon · Wonders
  • As designed

    Builders trained or purchased in a city with at least one World Wonder receive +1 build charge.

    Same mechanism as Guilds of the New Realm. One extra charge however many Wonders the city has; a captured Wonder city qualifies. A Wonder still under construction does not count: a script marks a city once its Wonder is finished.

Bigger deserts

Every desert the map generator lays out grows outward by one full ring of tiles, plus a ragged second ring in which each tile has a 50% chance. In testing on synthetic maps, deserts came out about three times larger.

  • Only grassland and plains next to an existing desert convert. Tundra, snow and water are never touched.
  • The number of deserts never goes up, though close neighbours can merge.
  • It runs during map generation, so rivers, floodplains, resources and start biases all see the larger deserts.
  • Gathering Storm ruleset only. Works with the stock map scripts and the BBS scripts that use the standard terrain step.

Great lakes

A few of the lakes the map generator places grow into great lakes of 6 to 9 tiles, so that Harbors have room and something to work. One lake is grown per 120 land tiles (at least three), and the ones chosen are as far apart as possible.

  • Only existing lakes grow. No new lakes are added.
  • A grown lake never touches the sea or another lake and never passes 9 tiles, the game's limit for a lake, so it keeps fresh water and can hold Discus Fish.
  • Rivers and their banks, volcanoes and natural wonders are never flooded.
  • It runs during map generation, so features, resources and start positions all see the larger lakes. More lake tiles also means more Discus Fish.
  • The Colossus, Great Lighthouse, Sydney Opera House and Torre de Belém can be built on lakes. Their other requirements are unchanged, and their in-game descriptions still say "Coast". The Statue of Liberty, Venetian Arsenal and Golden Gate Bridge still cannot.
  • Gathering Storm ruleset only. Works with map scripts that use the standard lake step, including stock and BBS Pangaea.

Renaming leaders

Leaders are named after their concept for now. To change one, edit the text of its LOC_LEADER_EUROPA_*_NAME row at the top of Text/Europa_Leaders_Text_en_US.xml and restart the game. Nothing else refers to the display name.
